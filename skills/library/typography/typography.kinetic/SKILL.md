# Kinetic Typography

- Typography must communicate first and decorate second.
- Use one dominant typeface and at most one supporting face per visual system.
- Animate phrase or word groups to match speech emphasis; avoid flashing every word without purpose.
- Maintain strong contrast, sensible line lengths and consistent safe margins.
- Never obscure faces, key objects or essential information.
- Check the rendered frame at phone size, not only on a desktop preview.
